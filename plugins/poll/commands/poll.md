---
description: Put a question on the projector now, or run a poll file you prepared earlier. Students answer anonymously from their own devices.
argument-hint: "[a question, or a poll file, or nothing]"
---

Invoke the `poll` skill and follow it, with "$ARGUMENTS" as the argument.

In short, and the skill has the detail:

- Nothing → bring the room up and put the join link, QR and room code on the
  projector, with no question loaded.
- Something that names a file → add that prepared poll file to the session.
- Anything else → it is a question. Turn it into one question of the right type
  and put it up now, voting open.

You are almost certainly in front of a class. Do not ask a clarifying question,
do not confirm before pushing, and do not talk through your reasoning — make the
call, put it up, and report in one line what went up and how to answer it. If
you read the question wrong, the instructor will type `/poll` again, which is
faster than any confirmation round trip would have been.
