Invoke the `poll` skill and follow it, with "$ARGUMENTS" as the question and
`wordcloud` as the type. Do not infer the type from the phrasing — the
instructor has already chosen it.

A cloud wants a short answer. If "$ARGUMENTS" doesn't already ask for one, put
"Short answer:" in front of it — sentences turn the cloud to mush.

Don't ask for a single word. Two or three words stay together as one entry, so
"interest rate risk" lands whole instead of being shredded into a bare "risk",
and students aren't forced to compress an idea into a word that doesn't fit it.

You are in front of a class. Don't confirm first and don't think out loud — push
it and report in one line what went up.
