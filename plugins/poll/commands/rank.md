description: Ask the class to put things in order and show them by average position.
argument-hint: "[the question, then the things to order]"
---

Invoke the `poll` skill and follow it, with "$ARGUMENTS" as the question and
`rank` as the type. Do not infer the type from the phrasing — the instructor
has already chosen it.

The things to order come from the list in "$ARGUMENTS", split on commas and a
trailing "or". Keep the labels short — they are read off a projector and dragged
on a phone. Ranking more than six things is a chore and the app refuses it.

Make sure the question says which direction the order runs — "from least to most
interest-rate risk" — or half the room will sort it backwards.

You are in front of a class. Don't confirm first and don't think out loud — push
it and report in one line what went up.
