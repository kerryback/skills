---
description: Ask the class to rate something on a scale — how confident, how clear, how solid they feel.
argument-hint: "[the question]"
---

Invoke the `survey` skill and follow it, with "$ARGUMENTS" as the question and
`scale` as the type. Do not infer the type from the phrasing — the instructor
has already chosen it.

Use 1 to 5 unless "$ARGUMENTS" names a different range. Give both ends a label
drawn from what is actually being asked — "Lost" and "Solid" for how well they
follow something, "Not at all" and "Completely" for agreement — because a naked
row of numbers doesn't tell a student which end is which.

Never set an `answer`. There is no right answer to how someone feels, and
marking one would hide the distribution while voting is open.

You are in front of a class. Don't confirm first and don't think out loud — push
it and report in one line what went up.
