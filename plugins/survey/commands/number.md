argument-hint: "[the question]"
---

Invoke the `survey` skill and follow it, with "$ARGUMENTS" as the question and
`number` as the type. Do not infer the type from the phrasing — the instructor
has already chosen it.

Set `unit` when the question implies one — "%" for a yield or a rate, "$" for a
price — so the mean and median read correctly on the projector.

Set `answer` when the true value is knowable and you know it, since the display
marks it on the histogram and that comparison is the whole point of asking. Say
in your reply what you used. If it is a live market number you cannot verify,
leave it out rather than guessing at one.

You are in front of a class. Don't confirm first and don't think out loud — push
it and report in one line what went up.
